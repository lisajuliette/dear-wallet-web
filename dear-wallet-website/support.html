Getting started

How do I log something?

Tap the round LOG button in the middle of the bottom bar, type the amount and what it was, then tap nvm!. Only those two are needed; category, note and photo are optional.

What's the difference between "didn't buy" and "loved it"?

"Didn't buy" adds to your kept total. "Loved it" is for things you bought and adored; they go to your loved list with an optional photo and never change your kept total.

I bought it after all. Now what?

Open the item and tap "i bought it after all". It leaves your tally, and "undo, i didn't buy it" brings it back. Rewards you've earned stay.

Moving to a new phone

Sign in on your old phone first (Settings › account) so your data is backed up. Then install Dear Wallet on the new phone, iPhone or Android, and sign in with the same Apple account or email. Everything, including your decorated diary pages, comes across.

If you never signed in, your data lives only on the old phone. Use Settings › export my data to keep a copy.

Export and delete

  • Export: Settings › export my data creates a CSV of your items and diary plus a zip of your photos, and opens the share sheet.
  • Delete your account: Settings › account › delete account. This removes your account, entries, diary and photos from our servers. It can't be undone.
  • Can't open the app? Email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

App lock

Turn on Settings › app lock to protect just the diary or the whole app with Face ID or fingerprint unlock, with a six-digit passcode as backup. Forgot the passcode? Tap "forgot passcode?" on the lock screen and confirm with your account to reset it.

Stickers and rewards

You get one star per day you use the app, and stars never reset. Stickers drop randomly as you log and write; if a few actions in a row don't drop one, the next one always does. Sticker packs unlock at milestones, and seasonal packs open during their season. Nothing can be bought.
