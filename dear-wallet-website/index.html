how it works

three taps, one lil win

Most money apps track what you spent. Dear Wallet keeps the moments you paused, and makes them feel like something.

  1. log the nvm

    Saw it, wanted it, walked away? Type how much it was and what it was. That's all it needs, and it works offline.

  2. get a lil reward

    Every day you show up earns a star on your calendar, and stickers drop as you go. The rare ones are holo.

  3. watch it add up

    Your tally grows by day, week and month. Point it at a goal and see how close you are to the thing you actually want.

PRIVACY.EXE

your diary is yours

  • No bank connection. Dear Wallet never asks for your bank, cards or transactions. You type what you want to remember.
  • No ads, no trackers. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app, and we never sell your data.
  • Lock it with Face ID or fingerprint. Lock just the diary or the whole app. Your face and fingerprint never leave your phone.
  • Start without an account. Sign in only if you want a backup and your diary on a new phone.
  • Export or delete anytime. Both live in settings, and delete means everything.

The full details are in plain English in our privacy policy.

questions

good to know

Is this a budgeting app?

Not really. Budgeting apps track what you spend. Dear Wallet keeps the things you didn't buy, so you can see how much those small pauses add up to.

Does it connect to my bank?

No, and it never will. You log things yourself, and only an amount and a name are needed.

What if I end up buying it anyway?

Tap "i bought it after all" on the item. It leaves your tally, nothing else changes, and you can undo it. Stars, stickers and milestones you earned stay yours.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start logging straight away. Sign in with Apple or email when you want a backup or want to use Dear Wallet on another phone, including switching between iPhone and Android.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Yes, both. Sign in on either and your tally, diary and stickers follow you, even if you switch from one to the other.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone first, then backed up to our database in the EU if you sign in. The privacy policy has the details.
