The short version

  • Dear Wallet works without an account. What you log stays on your phone until you choose to sign in.
  • If you sign in, we store your entries so you can back them up and use them on another phone.
  • We don't connect to your bank, show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, or sell your data.
  • You can export everything, or delete your account and all of its data, from settings in the app.

What we don't collect

  • Bank, card or transaction data. Dear Wallet never connects to financial accounts.
  • Face or fingerprint data. Biometric unlock (Face ID on iPhone, fingerprint or face unlock on Android) is handled by your phone's operating system; the app only learns whether unlocking succeeded, and only on your phone.
  • Your passcode. It is stored as a hash in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android) and never leaves it.
  • Location, contacts, advertising identifiers or browsing data.
  • Analytics or usage tracking. There are no analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.

Weekly check-in reminders are scheduled on your phone as local notifications; we don't run a push notification service for them.

Why we use it

  • To provide the app (performance of our agreement with you, GDPR article 6(1)(b)): saving what you log, syncing across your phones, restoring your data when you sign in, and calculating totals, rewards and milestones.
  • To keep the service secure (legitimate interest, article 6(1)(f)): preventing abuse of our servers and fixing errors. Our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses on requests, for this.
  • To answer you (legitimate interest): when you email us, we use your message and address to reply.

We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions, and we don't read your diary.

Where it's stored

Data you sync is stored with Supabase, our database and file storage provider, in their [EU region, e.g. Frankfurt, Germany] region. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each account can only read its own rows and photos; this is enforced by the database itself.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your data. We only use these service providers, under data processing agreements:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and file storage.
  • Apple: if you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple confirms your identity and gives us your email or a relay address.
  • Apple and Google: the App Store and Google Play handle downloads and app updates under their own privacy policies. We receive no personal data about you from them.

If a provider processes data outside the EEA, transfers are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision. We may disclose data if Dutch or EU law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Synced data is kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account in settings, your entries, diary, photos and account are deleted from our database and storage straight away. Encrypted backups held by our provider roll off within [30] days.
  • Data that was never synced lives only on your phone and is removed when you delete the app.
  • Emails to support are kept for up to two years, then deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, and you can object to or restrict how we use it. Most of this you can do yourself in the app:

  • Export: Settings › export my data gives you your items and diary as CSV and your photos as a zip.
  • Delete: Settings › account › delete account removes your account and everything in it.

For anything else, email hello@dearwallet.app and we'll reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or the authority where you live.

Children

Dear Wallet is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
